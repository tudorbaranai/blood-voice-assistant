How Blood works

Every time you talk to Blood, your words go on a short trip through a chain of small programs, each doing one job. This chain is called the pipeline. Pick a scenario and press Next to follow one request, step by step, through your Asus.

What the brain actually receives

When Home Assistant asks Ollama a question, it doesn't send just your sentence. It stacks four layers into one message. This is why the system prompt matters, and why it isn't the only thing the model reads.

1. Your system prompt (the one we wrote and tested)Who Blood is, how to speak, the calendar, the honesty rules. You edit this in the Ollama agent's settings.
2. Home Assistant's hidden partAdded automatically when "Control Home Assistant" is on: the list of exposed devices, which room you're in, and the tools the AI may use. You can't edit this, so our prompt must not contradict it.
3. The conversation so farThe last few back-and-forths, so "the ceiling one" makes sense after "turn on the lamp".
4. What you just saidThe text Whisper heard, e.g. "what is the capital of Peru".

Offline vs online: what would change?

Try the Online (cloud) button above. The ear, brain and voice would be rented from companies on the internet instead of running on your Asus. The pipeline's shape stays exactly the same. Only who does each job changes.

The best of both: an offline brain with online data

🌦️ The trick

Home Assistant can fetch small pieces of data from the internet by itself, like the weather forecast (free, no account). It saves the forecast locally. When you ask "will it rain?", Blood reads the saved forecast.

🔒 What leaves the house

Only "send me the forecast for my city". Never your voice, never your questions. The brain, ear and voice stay 100% offline.

🧩 Does it change our plan?

No. The pipeline is modular, like LEGO: each box is one dropdown in Settings → Voice assistants. We can add a data feed or swap one box later without rebuilding anything.

Different idea, also called "online": using Blood or Home Assistant from your phone when you're away from home. That's remote access. It doesn't touch the pipeline at all. Safe options are a private tunnel (e.g. Tailscale, free) or Home Assistant Cloud (paid). The one thing never to do: open a port on your router to the whole internet.

Mini glossary

Pipeline
A chain of steps where each one's output is the next one's input: sound → text → answer → sound.
Wake word
The phrase ("Hey Blood") a tiny always-on program listens for. Nothing else is processed until it hears it.
STT / TTS
Speech-to-text (Whisper, the ear) and text-to-speech (Piper, the voice).
LLM
Large language model: the AI brain (qwen3). It only turns text into text.
Integration
A plug-in that connects Home Assistant to something else: Ollama, Hue bulbs, the weather...
VM
Virtual machine: a pretend computer running inside your real one. Home Assistant lives in it.
NAT / port
The VM sits behind VirtualBox like a house behind a gate. A port is a numbered door; we forwarded Windows door 8123 to the VM's door 80.
10.0.2.2
The address the VM uses to reach Windows itself. That's how Home Assistant finds Ollama.
System prompt
The job description the brain reads before every question.
Temperature
How adventurous the brain is when picking words. Low = consistent, high = creative.
