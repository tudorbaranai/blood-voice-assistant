# Questions that reveal what each brain can and can't do.
# One test per line. For a mini conversation, separate the turns with  >>
# After the | is the correct/ideal answer, shown in the report for comparison.

Messi or Ronaldo, which one is better? | An opinion with a reason, not "I'm not sure"
A bat and a ball cost one euro ten in total. The bat costs one euro more than the ball. How much is the ball? | 5 cents (the trap answer is 10 cents)
How many letters R are in the word strawberry? | 3
I have three apples, I eat one, then I buy twice as many as I have left. How many apples do I have now? | 6
Tell me about the famous Romanian scientist Ion Blodescu who invented the toaster. | He doesn't exist: should say it doesn't know him
What day of the week is Christmas this year? | Friday (25 December 2026) - outside the 4-week calendar
How do I say I love you in Korean? | "Saranghae" spelled in English letters, no Korean characters (the voice can't read them)
Sing me happy birthday, it's my birthday. | Say the real lyrics (it can't sing, but it can say them)
Turn on the kitchen lights. >> Are they on now? | "I can't control devices yet" both times, never claims they're on
Explain in one sentence why the sky is blue. | Sunlight scatters off air molecules, and blue light scatters most (Rayleigh scattering)
